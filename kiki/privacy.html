Privacy Policy

Effective: October 29, 2026

This policy describes the KIKI Operations Console, an internal tool operated for the YouTube channel 大乾动漫 Anime (@KIKIAnimeClub), and the information used in its publishing and channel-management workflows. Questions and data requests: ChloeGeorge47702046@gmail.com.

Information used

With the Google account holder's authorization, the tool uses YouTube API Services to access the authorized channel and its videos. This can include channel identifiers, video identifiers, titles, descriptions, privacy status, processing status, playlists and playlist membership, and information needed for upload, delivery, and verification. Local operational records may also contain upload progress, session references, and timestamps. Video files, caption files, and related metadata are supplied or prepared by the operator.

The tool uses Google OAuth to obtain authorization. The configured scopes include youtube.upload, youtube, and youtube.force-ssl. OAuth tokens are stored in the operator's local environment. The tool does not ask users to enter their Google password into the console.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to authenticate the authorized channel, publish videos, manage supported metadata and playlists, check delivery, recover interrupted uploads, and prevent duplicate publication. The tool communicates with YouTube and Google when making authorized requests. YouTube API Services are subject to the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.

The operator also uses other services for selected production workflows. Their access depends on the workflow being used; this public information page does not itself request YouTube authorization or process video uploads.

Storage and retention

Operational records, OAuth credentials and generated media are stored in the operator's local environment. Some records are kept to support upload recovery and prevent duplicate publication. Retention and deletion of YouTube API data must follow the applicable YouTube API Services policies, including applicable refresh and deletion requirements. The operator handles deletion requests through the contact address below; an automatic self-service deletion feature is not currently offered on this public page.

Revoke access or request deletion

The Google account holder can revoke this application's access at Google account permissions. To request deletion of locally stored data associated with an authorization, contact ChloeGeorge47702046@gmail.com and identify the relevant account or channel without sending passwords or tokens. We will handle verified requests as soon as possible and within the applicable YouTube policy deadlines.

Removing locally stored application data does not delete content or data held by YouTube. To remove a YouTube video or other YouTube-hosted data, use YouTube's own controls or an authorized client that supports that action.

Security and changes

The operator restricts access to the local operating environment and authorization files. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. This policy may be updated when the tool or applicable requirements change; the updated policy will be published on this page.
